Getting Started — 3 Steps

1

Navigate to E*TRADE

Go to your E*TRADE stock plan page. The extension activates automatically on that page — no setup required.

Open E*TRADE Stock Plans
2

View Your RSU Holdings

The extension automatically adds IL Tax, Net Proceeds, and Rate columns to your sellable holdings table. Type a quantity in the Qty to Sell field to see real-time calculations update instantly.

3

Click Any IL Tax Cell

Click any value in the IL Tax column to see a full breakdown: ordinary income vs. capital gains split, ILS amounts at the current exchange rate, and an easy Copy button to paste into a spreadsheet or report.

The 2-Year Rule (Section 102)

Israeli tax treatment depends on how long ago the RSUs were granted:

≥ 2 years from grant date Capital gains track — 25% flat tax on the full gain.
< 2 years from grant date Ordinary income track — marginal rates apply to the full proceeds.
