Configuration Popup — Suggested Parameters

Click items to select which ones you want included. You can suggest others below.

✓

Sell price override

Default: current market price. Override with a custom limit price (applied to all grants). Shown prominently at the top.

✓

Ordinary income tax rate

Default 47%. Used for grants held less than 2 years post-vesting (marginal rate on the full gain).

✓

Capital gains tax rate

Default 25%. Used for grants held 2+ years post-vesting (applied to gain above FMV at vesting).

+

High-earner surtax (mas yeser)

Optional 3% additional tax on gains, for income above the annual threshold (~₪700k). Toggle on/off.

+

Brokerage commission / fees

Flat fee or per-share fee deducted from net proceeds. Useful for accurate net calculation.

+

Display currency (USD / ILS)

Toggle between showing amounts in USD or ILS. If ILS selected, enter a USD/ILS exchange rate.
