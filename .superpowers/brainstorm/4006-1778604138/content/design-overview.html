Architecture

📄 content.js
  • Runs on E*TRADE stock plan page
  • Parses #stockplanjson → FMV at vesting, vesting dates, market price per grant
  • MutationObserver waits for React to render the sellable table
  • Injects IL Tax & Net columns
  • Watches quantity inputs → recalculates on change
  • Listens for settings messages from popup
⚙️ popup.html / popup.js
  • Sell price override (default: market)
  • Ordinary income rate (default 47%)
  • Capital gains rate (default 25%)
  • High-earner surtax toggle (+3%)
  • Currency: USD / ILS + exchange rate
  • Saves to chrome.storage.sync
  • Sends message to content script on change
🧮 Tax Logic
Cost basis = FMV at vesting (already income-taxed)
Gain = (sellPrice − FMV) × qty
≥ 2yr post-vesting:
Tax = gain × 25% (+3% if surtax)
< 2yr post-vesting:
Tax = gain × 47% (+3% if surtax)
Net = (sellPrice × qty) − Tax
